Quick tutorial

Each dot is one geocoded HCAD property account.

1. Look at a property

  • Hover a dot for a quick preview (address, account #, value).
  • Click a dot to pin its details. The popup stays open until you click the map or the ×, so you can select and copy any text.
  • Use Copy next to Acct to copy the account number, then paste it into the HCAD or Harris County Tax Office account search. Account numbers are always shown as 13 characters (padded with leading zeros).
  • Dots are hard to hit when zoomed out. Zoom in, or just click near a dot; hit areas are larger than the dots themselves.

2. Filter

  • Narrow by Protested, Present in 2024, Market value, Year of improvement, Zip code and Exemptions.
  • Matching filters at the top shows how many properties remain. Choosing a zip code also zooms to it.

3. Change how it looks

  • View: individual points or a density heatmap. Cluster points groups nearby dots into numbered bubbles.
  • Use the layer control (top-left) for Streets, Aerial, Aerial + labels or Topographic basemaps.
  • Buttons under the zoom: ⌂ reset to Harris County, ◎ my location, ⛶ fullscreen.

4. Screenshots of the whole county

  • Click « in the panel header to collapse the panel (and its legend); click ☰ Panel to bring it back.
  • Press H to hide all overlays (panel, tab, credit box) for a clean shot; press H again to restore.
  • Press the ⌂ button first to frame all of Harris County.

Shortcuts

  • P collapse / expand the panel   H clean view   ? this help   Esc close help
